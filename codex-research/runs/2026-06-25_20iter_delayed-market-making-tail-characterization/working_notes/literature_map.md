# Literature Map

## Full-Information Delayed Online Learning

- Joulani, Gyorgy, and Szepesvari (ICML/PMLR 2013) is the primary black-box delayed-feedback reduction. Verified source states the ICML 2013/PMLR venue and frames delay effects on regret; use this for the finite-grid delayed-learning step, after explicitly mapping public price observations to delayed full-information reward vectors.
- Joulani, Gyorgy, and Szepesvari (AAAI 2016), "Delay-Tolerant Online Convex Optimization," is verified. The source states a black-box reduction from delayed-feedback to standard online learning for full-information OCO and adaptive-gradient algorithms. Useful for horizon-free/adaptive variants of the grid reduction.
- Quanrud and Khashabi (NeurIPS/NIPS 2015) gives adversarial-delay regret in terms of total delay \(D\), with source page reporting \(O(\sqrt D)\)-type control. This supports bounded-delay scaling via \(D\lesssim TH\), but still needs model mapping.
- Weinberger and Ordentlich (IEEE TIT 2002) is an older fixed-delay prediction reference, now verified through DBLP/DOI metadata. Useful background for individual-sequence prediction with delayed observations; not specific to market-making fills.
- Van der Hoeven and Cesa-Bianchi (AISTATS/PMLR 2022) treats arm-dependent delays in nonstochastic bandits and experts. Verified source says the full-information experts setting has first-order bounds depending on delays/losses of the best arm. Relevant because quote delays are arm-dependent.

## Unrestricted, Skipping, And Aggregate Delay Bounds

- Thune, Cesa-Bianchi, and Seldin (NeurIPS 2019) handles nonstochastic bandits with unbounded delays and skipping excessively delayed observations; verified source gives bounds involving total delay and skip/oracle quantities. Useful for replacing a hard \(H\) truncation by adaptive skipping.
- Zimmert and Seldin (AISTATS 2020/arXiv:1910.06054) gives optimal arbitrary-delay adversarial bandit bounds with refined excluded-round sets. Verified through arXiv; relevance is conceptual for skip-set tail penalties.
- Lancewicki, Segal, Koren, and Mansour (ICML/PMLR 2021) studies stochastic bandits with unrestricted and infinite delays, with quantile-based delay dependence. Verified source; relevant for quantile alternatives to uniform tails.
- Zierahn, van der Hoeven, Lancewicki, Rosenberg, and Cesa-Bianchi (JMLR 2025) gives a modern unified analysis for delayed nonstochastic bandit feedback. Verified source; useful terminology but less direct because the truncated market-making reduction is full-information.
- Bistritz, Zhou, Chen, Bambos, and Blanchet (NeurIPS 2019) studies delayed adversarial bandit feedback and missing feedback beyond horizon. Verified source; useful for horizon-censoring distinctions, not direct proof support.
- Bistritz, Zhou, Chen, Bambos, and Blanchet (JMLR 2022), "No Weighted-Regret Learning in Adversarial Bandits with Delays," is verified. It studies delayed adversarial bandits with weighted-regret objectives and bounds involving total delay; relevant background for horizon-weighted finite-prefix dual certificates, not a delayed-fill characterization theorem.
- Gyorgy and Joulani (ICML/PMLR 2021) is now verified. It gives adversarial bandit bounds adapting to observed delays/data and includes skip-set bounds. Relevant for replacing a fixed truncation horizon by adaptive skipping; not directly full-information market making.
- Flaspohler et al. (ICML/PMLR 2021) is now verified. It reduces delayed online learning to optimism and may help treat predictable delayed fills as hints rather than worst-case lost mass.
- Esposito, Masoudian, Qiu, van der Hoeven, Cesa-Bianchi, and Seldin (ICML/PMLR 2023) is verified. It studies delayed bandits with intermediate observations; relevant as an analogue for public price history that may make some delayed fills predictable.
- Ryabchenko, Attias, and Roy (arXiv 2026) is verified as a recent reduction from delayed to immediate OCO with total-delay/outstanding-observation terms. Treat as a current candidate for adaptive truncation ideas, not as an established market-making characterization.
- Masoudian, Zimmert, and Seldin (NeurIPS/OpenReview 2024) is verified. Its abstract explicitly frames the complexity of delayed bandits in terms of information missing at decision time, measured by outstanding observations, rather than elapsed delay. This is conceptually well aligned with replacing raw \(\beta\)-tails by missing-information or skipped-feedback quantities.
- Schlisselberg, Lancewicki, Auer, and Mansour (NeurIPS 2025/arXiv:2505.24193) is verified. It gives best-of-both-worlds delayed-bandit bounds with terms involving cumulative delay and maximum missing observations. Useful for adaptive-delay context, but not direct support for the public-price full-information market-making reduction.
- Ryabchenko, Attias, and Roy (COLT/PMLR 2025) is verified. It studies capacity-constrained delayed online learning and reports full-information bounds with total-delay/tracking-capacity terms. Relevant to adaptive truncation and bounded outstanding-fill bookkeeping.
- Masoudian, Zimmert, and Seldin (NeurIPS/OpenReview 2022/arXiv:2206.14906) is verified as the earlier best-of-both-worlds delayed-bandit paper. It includes arbitrary-delay bounds using total delay, maximal delay, and outstanding observations, and is the predecessor to the 2024 robustness-to-excessive-delays work already in the map.
- Ryabchenko, Attias, and Roy (arXiv:2606.11711, 2026), "Capacity-Constrained Online Convex Optimization with Delayed Feedback," is verified. It extends capacity-constrained delayed learning to OCO/BCO and delayed-weighted reductions. Relevant to adaptive truncation, tracking-capacity, and outstanding-information sufficiency, not to raw-tail necessity.
- Heliou, Mertikopoulos, and Zhou (ICML/PMLR 2020), "Gradient-free Online Learning in Continuous Games with Delayed Rewards," is verified. It studies continuous-action payoff-based online learning with delayed, asynchronous rewards and priority-queue processing. Useful for adaptive delayed-reward terminology and continuous-action context, but not a theorem for suffix-horizon market fills.

## Current Takeaway

Existing delayed-learning literature supports sufficiency via bounded, total, adaptive, optimistic, skipped, outstanding-observation, or intermediate-observation delay terms. It does not support necessity of the unweighted \(\beta\) tail. Standard online-learning references also reinforce that the meaningful lower-bound regime is usually universal/minimax or simultaneous-prefix, not horizon- and law-specific oracle policies. Iteration 4's accepted concatenation theorems are direct elementary lower bounds, with the cited unknown-horizon/anytime literature serving as terminology and contrast. The literature is more aligned with aggregate, quantile, best-arm, skipped, value-of-information, horizon-censored, censored-threshold, or adaptive-regret quantities, but the counterexamples show raw delayed reward mass is not necessary for instance-wise learnability unless tied to unpredictability, prefix incompatibility, or information acquisition.

Iteration 6 verifies that the multi-threshold \(\kappa\) LP aligns with classical posted-price/equal-revenue geometry: local gaps are welfare minus monopoly revenue and are capped by \(1/e\). The simultaneous-approachability and U-calibration sources are analogies for one action sequence satisfying several objectives. None of these external sources supplies a global delayed market-making characterization; the accepted lower bounds remain internal prefix-indistinguishability arguments.

Iteration 7 sharpens the internal threshold geometry: the exact \(m\)-positive-support local gap is \(((m-1)/m)^m\), approaching \(1/e\). The new price-discrimination/robust-pricing sources above are useful context for equal-revenue and minimax duality, but the finite-support constant and the terminal-block \(1/e\) universal lift should currently be cited as internal results, not as imported theorems.

Iteration 8 promotes the same-path multi-threshold anytime lift internally; no direct external theorem was found for this delayed-market-making statement. The new calibration/U-calibration sources are decision-relevant simultaneous-objective analogies only. They should be cited, if at all, to motivate replacing raw tail conditions by payoff/actionability moduli, not as proof support for CL-026/CL-027.

Iteration 9 adds two internal results: the exact finite-\(C\) local root formula \(\kappa(C)=\delta_*(C)\), and the universal equal-revenue \(1/e\) upper bound for exogenous horizon-oblivious paths. A narrow literature check found robust/minimax-pricing analogies but no direct source for the exact root formula or the delayed-market-making \(1/e\) theorem. Treat Bergemann-Schlag 2011 and Caldentey-Liu-Lobel 2017 as adjacent context only, and Anagnostides et al. 2026 as a weak approachability analogy only.

Iteration 10 adds an internal deterministic derandomization of the known-path \(1/e\) upper bound. The external literature supports only the proof technology: Hoeffding for concentration, probabilistic-method references for nonconstructive existence, and empirical-process/bracketing sources for optional rate improvements. No direct delayed-market-making theorem was found for this derandomization, and the result remains separate from the false original tail-characterization conjecture.

The robust/minimax-pricing sources above remain adjacent analogies for randomized pricing, finite approximation, and LP/minimax structure; no external source found in the iteration reports proves the compact strict-crossing menu formula or the delayed-market-making suffix theorem. The alternating-path stress test confirms the suffix-menu condition is sufficient only.

Literature checks found no direct theorem for this delayed-fill characterization. Lagziel-Lehrer 2015 is relevant delayed-approachability background for future vector-deficit formulations; Bistritz et al. 2022 is relevant weighted-regret delayed-bandit background for horizon-weighted certificates; Xie-Zhu-Shishkin 2025 is adjacent pricing context. All three should be cited as background only.
