# Monotone Gluing From D_n

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Try to prove a market-specific gluing theorem: for deterministic exogenous known paths, D_n(P)=o(n) plus monotonicity of each suffix maximum M_{t,T} in T implies existence of one horizon-oblivious marginal sequence with o(T) regret. Focus on a scale-stability lemma showing finite-prefix solutions can be modified so all dyadic older horizons keep sublinear deficits.

## Why Promising
This is the current sharp open target identified by the solver, and the abstract parity obstruction is not obviously realizable by monotone market suffix maxima.

## Risks
The finite-prefix optimizer may still sacrifice old horizons in a way monotonicity cannot repair; the proof may collapse back to the already accepted fixed-envelope compactness criterion.

## Suggested Lenses
- proof strategy
- finite LP duality
- compactness
