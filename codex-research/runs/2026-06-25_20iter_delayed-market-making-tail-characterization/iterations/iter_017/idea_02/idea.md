# Unequal-Low Droughts

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Study a single drought with unequal lows, h^M,0^{N_0},ell_1,0^{N_1},ell_2,...,0^{N_r},ell_r,0^L,h. First restrict to two or three distinct low levels and derive the reduced action menu {ell_j^-, h^-}. Look for a PAVA-like pooling rule or for a minimal counterexample where an earlier low horizon binds even though the last low has the largest raw surplus.

## Why Promising
The equal-low proof's monotonicity is the fragile point; unequal lows are the next place genuine front-loading constraints should appear if the two-level recursion has real structure.

## Risks
Unequal lows may be too broad for a closed form; small examples may show several active horizons and no simple last-low formula.

## Suggested Lenses
- finite-dimensional LP derivation
- counterexample search
