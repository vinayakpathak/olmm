# Two-Low Active-Cap Formula

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Complete the two-low increasing theorem for h^M,0^{N0},ell,0^{N1},u,0^L,h with 0<ell<u<h, including all capacity-active cases. Start from the solver's non-saturated formula, then classify regimes where d1/ell>B0 or d1/ell+(d2-d1)_+/u>B0+B1 at the fixed point. For every regime, produce matching primal masses and dual weights.

## Why Promising
This is the smallest complete unequal-low theorem beyond CL-049 and would provide a reliable regression target for any proposed pooling or flow rule.

## Risks
The formula may have many boundary regimes, especially when S1, S2 and capacities make feasibility constraints active; the final statement may be too case-heavy to be conceptually useful.

## Suggested Lenses
- finite case proof
- primal-dual certificates
- capacity-active regimes
