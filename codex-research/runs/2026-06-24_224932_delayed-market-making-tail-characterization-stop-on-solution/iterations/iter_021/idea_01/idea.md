# Close Borel No-Gap Bridge

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Complete the original Borel-policy no-gap bridge under the exact finite-grid public-exogenous seed/state model. Prove both inclusions between common Borel randomized policies and completed public-prefix kernels: forward via seed/prefix induction and Fubini; reverse via Borel versions of completed kernels, simplex repair on null sets, and finite-action randomization.

## Why Promising
This is one of the few remaining gaps in an otherwise coherent finite/no-gap theorem package. A successful explorer could turn a conditional partial result into a referee-ready theorem with clear scope.

## Risks
This may only produce theorem hygiene rather than new insight. The bridge still depends on standard measurable-version facts and can easily overclaim behavioral equivalence instead of reward-coordinate equality.

- proof strategy
- measure-theory audit
