# Referee-Proof ba Dual

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Write the finitely additive finite-grid dual as a standalone proposition and stress-test it on known infinite-class gap examples. Verify the norm-separation proof, positivity of the separator, normalization to a finitely additive probability, and the relation between finite-prior/product-closure value and true ba value.

## Why Promising
The solver attempt relies on this as the exact arbitrary-class finite-grid value. A precise proof plus worked examples would clarify exactly what the abstract characterization buys and where it stops.

## Risks
Even if fully proved, it remains an abstract value identity, not a structural tail condition. It may not advance rates unless paired with examples where the ba witness can be computed.
