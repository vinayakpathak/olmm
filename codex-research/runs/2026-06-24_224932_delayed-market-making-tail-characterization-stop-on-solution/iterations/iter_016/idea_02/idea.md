# Close Borel No-Gap

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Finish the original Borel-policy no-gap theorem under payoff-weighted reward-measure domination. Prove or cite the completed-measurable-to-Borel version lemma, stochastic-kernel randomization lemma, weak-star closed simplex constraints, and ensure the same policy class is used for finite subclasses and the full class.

## Why Promising
The completed L-infinity kernel theorem is essentially accepted; closing this bridge would make the infinite-class no-gap result much more theorem-ready.

## Risks
The implementation may preserve only reward coordinates, not behavior; if the original policy model allows law-by-law versions, private feedback, or action effects, the theorem may fail or need stronger domination.

## Suggested Lenses
- measure theory
- proof strategy
