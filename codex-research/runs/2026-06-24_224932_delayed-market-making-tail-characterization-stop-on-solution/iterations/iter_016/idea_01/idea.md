ID: idea_01

## Summary
Turn the solver's finite-class/product-closed characterization into a referee-style theorem package: finite grid identity, positive-part continuum approximation with G_T, product-closed infinite-class extension, and the exact public-exogenous randomized policy assumptions. Check every quantifier and whether the solver overclaims beyond these hypotheses.

## Why Promising
This is the closest thing to a necessary-and-sufficient theorem already in the notes; a precise audit can determine whether it is a valid partial solution or still has a fatal gap.

## Risks
This may only certify the already-known finite/no-gap result and still leave the original scalar-tail goal open. Hidden gaps could remain in the policy class, sign convention, or grid-to-continuum comparison.
