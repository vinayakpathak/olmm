# Infinite-Class No-Duality-Gap Test

ID: idea_01

## Summary
For infinite public-exogenous classes, investigate whether the finite-subclass modulus Phi_T equals the full grid minimax value under natural compactness or continuity assumptions. Work on fixed finite grid G_T first, define the attainable reward set in l_infty(Theta_T), and either prove a no-duality-gap theorem or construct a strict compactness-gap example.

## Why Promising
This is the most direct route from the accepted finite-class characterization to a genuine unknown-class characterization.

## Risks
The dual of l_infty may introduce finitely additive priors; measurable policy compactness may fail; the needed assumptions may be too strong to cover natural classes.

## Suggested Lenses
- functional-analytic minimax
- compactness counterexample search
- measurable policy topology
