# q-Scaled Tail Lower Bounds

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Complete the q-scaled hidden-terminal family. Define the no-reveal event exactly, compute beta, Delta, delta, and the Bayes gap, then test whether horizon-indexed classes with prescribed tail profile can force regret of order qD or produce power-tail lower rates matching the truncation upper bound.

## Why Promising
It is the most concrete route from the known finite-horizon lower-bound atom to quantitative minimax rates in terms of valuable delayed tail mass.

## Risks
Naive repetition can erase the gap because the comparator is a single fixed quote in expectation. The construction must avoid the iid block-splicing failure already recorded in the notes.

## Suggested Lenses
- finite-horizon lower bound
- rate matching
- counterexample search
