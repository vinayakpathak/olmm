# Bayes-Gap Delay Modulus

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Generalize the hidden-terminal construction into a Bayes-gap delay modulus: for a class C_T, find pairs or mixtures of exogenous laws whose observable prefixes, or H-truncated reward feedback, are indistinguishable for many rounds but whose best fixed quote reward curves are separated. Prove a Yao/Le Cam lower bound in terms of the accumulated Bayes gap.

## Why Promising
This targets the real obstruction identified by the solver attempt: valuable delayed reward plus learner ignorance, not raw hitting delay. It could become the necessary counterpart to the delta_T(H) upper theorem.

## Risks
The resulting object may be close to the full minimax value and therefore hard to compute. It may also fail to yield clean scalar tail conditions unless extra closure or richness assumptions are imposed on the class.

## Suggested Lenses
- proof strategy
- information-complexity lower bound
- model taxonomy
