# Decision-theoretic shortcut

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Find the cleanest finite statistical decision theorem that implies the identity: finite parameter set, standard Borel observations/histories, finite action grid at each time, randomized decision kernels, bounded loss. Translate the delayed-market problem into that theorem and identify the Bayes risk term using the already accepted fixed-prior myopic lemma.

## Why Promising
This could replace the solver's informal compactness proof with a standard minimax/least-favorable-prior result, making the final write-up shorter and more defensible.

## Risks
A classical theorem may require assumptions not present here, such as finite observation spaces, dominated experiments, compact action spaces with continuity, or nonnegative loss conventions. It may also prove only epsilon-minimax or value existence, not the exact displayed Psi_G formula.
