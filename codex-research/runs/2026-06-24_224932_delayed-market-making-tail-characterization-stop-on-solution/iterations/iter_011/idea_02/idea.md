# Borel policy alignment

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Write the original-policy alignment theorem for finite grids. Define admissible private-randomized Borel public-price policies exactly, prove by seed induction that a_t = alpha_t(P_{1:t}, U_{1:t}), and derive law-independent public-prefix kernels q_{t,a}(h) preserving all L_theta reward coordinates.

## Why Promising
It is the main missing step for upgrading the completed L-infinity no-gap theorem under payoff-weighted domination to the intended original policy class.

## Risks
This is measure-theoretic and could get bogged down in policy-model choices. The result should preserve expected reward vectors only, not pathwise behavior.

## Suggested Lenses
- proof strategy
- measurability audit
