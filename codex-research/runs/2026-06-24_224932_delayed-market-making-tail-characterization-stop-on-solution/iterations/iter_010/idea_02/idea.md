# Kernel implementation lemma

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Starting from a completed L-infinity(lambda_t; Delta(G)) prefix kernel, determine exact assumptions under which it has an admissible Borel or completed-measurable implementation preserving all payoff-weighted integrals.

## Why Promising
It isolates the reverse alignment issue and could make the infinite-class domination theorem referee-ready or identify the exact model restriction needed.

## Risks
Borel representatives may not exist simultaneously for all dominating measures without changing the policy class; the clean theorem may need to be stated on completed measurable spaces only.

## Suggested Lenses
- proof strategy
- counterexample search
