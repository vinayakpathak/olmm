# Policy-to-kernel alignment

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Prove that every admissible private-randomized Borel policy in the public exogenous model induces law-independent public-prefix marginal kernels q_t(P_1:t) on a finite quote grid, with identical expected reward coordinates L_theta.

## Why Promising
This is the main remaining gap between the accepted completed L-infinity kernel no-gap theorem and the original policy model.

## Risks
May fail if the original policy model lets delayed trade feedback carry information not already contained in public prices and private randomness; null-set conventions could force a completed-measure rather than Borel statement.

## Suggested Lenses
- proof strategy
- measure-theory audit
