# Natural Infinite-Class Gap

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Search for a natural public-exogenous infinite class where every finite subclass has small grid minimax value but the full class has positive or linear minimax value. Avoid relying on non-Borel labels if possible; otherwise prove that any standard-Borel attempt must fail under identifiable or dominated assumptions.

## Why Promising
It would clarify whether product closedness is a real mathematical assumption or mostly a technical artifact of the current proof.

## Risks
Actual market reward sets may be too structured to realize the abstract finite-support pathology. Countable or Borel-identifiable versions may be easy for universal policies, collapsing the obstruction.

## Suggested Lenses
- counterexample search
- model realization
- measurability audit
