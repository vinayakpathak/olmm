# Referee-Proof Dominated No-Gap Theorem

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Make the dominated-prefix infinite-class theorem referee-ready: for fixed finite T and finite G, assume all prefix laws are dominated by sigma-finite measures lambda_t, reduce randomized policies to public-prefix kernels q_t(h) in Delta(G), prove weak-* compactness of the feasible kernel set, weak-* continuity of reward coordinates, and product closedness of the attainable reward set.

## Why Promising
This would turn the solver's main finite-prior characterization into a real infinite-class theorem under checkable hypotheses, removing the largest formal gap in the current 'maybe solved' status.

## Risks
Measurable-version issues may be harder than they look; common domination may be too restrictive for the most interesting deterministic or singular classes.

## Suggested Lenses
- proof strategy
- measurability audit
- functional analysis
