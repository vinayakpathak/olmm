# Natural Infinite-Class No-Gap Conditions

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Search for no-gap hypotheses beyond common domination, such as compact parametric classes with continuity of theta -> E_theta rewards for every prefix kernel, tight dominated approximations, or Feller-style conditional laws. In parallel, try to build a natural nondominated market class where finite-subclass Bayes gaps miss the full minimax value.

## Why Promising
Common domination is sufficient but narrow. A more natural compactness criterion would make the value characterization useful for realistic infinite classes, while a clean counterexample would clarify the exact boundary.

## Risks
May collapse into abstract compactness assumptions that are hard to verify in market instances; nondominated deterministic classes may genuinely have gaps.

## Suggested Lenses
- generalization
- counterexample search
- topology of policies
