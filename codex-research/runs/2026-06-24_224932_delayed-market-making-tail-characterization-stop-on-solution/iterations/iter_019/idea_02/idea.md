# ba Dual vs Product Closure

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Compare the finitely additive dual with the existing product-closure finite-subclass formula. Check whether ba priors exactly represent separation points in the product closure of C, whether the dual recovers the non-Borel label gap, and whether product-closed or payoff-measure dominated classes force the ba supremum to be approximable by ordinary finite priors.

## Why Promising
It connects the new arbitrary-class proposal to accepted notes. This can clarify whether the dual is genuinely a true infinite-class theorem or just a restatement of the product-closure relaxation.

## Risks
The result may collapse to known product-closure facts and add little. Finitely additive priors can hide measurability pathologies, so the explorer must separate true policy gaps from artifacts of the dual space.
