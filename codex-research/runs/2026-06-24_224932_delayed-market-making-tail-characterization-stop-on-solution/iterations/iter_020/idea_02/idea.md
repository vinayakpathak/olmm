# Borel Policy No-Gap Bridge

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Complete the payoff-measure dominated no-gap theorem for the original Borel policy model, not just completed L-infinity kernels. Prove both inclusions C_Borel subset C_Linfty and C_Linfty subset C_Borel under the common Borel seed/state model, using Borel versions, finite-action randomization, and domination of payoff-weighted measures.

## Why Promising
This would turn the most useful interpretable infinite-class theorem from a conditional completed-kernel result into a usable result for ordinary policies.

## Risks
Could become theorem hygiene rather than new insight, and standard measurable-version facts may have hidden null-set pitfalls. It still will not handle continuum quotes or action-dependent prices.

## Suggested Lenses
- measure theory
- proof strategy
