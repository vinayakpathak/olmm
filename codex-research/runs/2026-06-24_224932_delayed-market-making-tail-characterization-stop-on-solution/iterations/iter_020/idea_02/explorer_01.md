## Summary

Under the exact finite-grid, public-exogenous, common Borel seed/state policy model, the Borel policy bridge looks closable as a reward-coordinate theorem:
$$
C_{\rm Borel}=C_{L^\infty}.
$$

The equality should be stated only for expected additive reward vectors, not pathwise behavior or joint action laws. The key measure-theoretic point is that payoff-measure domination is enough for the reverse inclusion: Borelization may change behavior on $\lambda_t$-null histories, but all payoff integrals are unchanged because $\mu^\theta_{t,a}\ll\lambda_t$.

## Concrete Progress

A clean theorem target is:

Fix finite $T$, finite grid $G$, standard Borel prefix spaces $X_t=[0,1]^t$, public exogenous laws $P^\theta$, and
$$
M_t=\max_{t<s\le T}P_s.
$$

Define finite payoff-weighted measures
$$
\mu^\theta_{t,a}(B)
=
\mathbb E_\theta[
\mathbf{1}\{P_{1:t}\in B\}\,a\mathbf{1}\{M_t>a\}
].
$$

Assume for each $t$ there is a sigma-finite Borel $\lambda_t$ such that $\mu^\theta_{t,a}\ll\lambda_t$ for all $\theta,a\in G$.

Then the reward vectors generated by common Borel finite-grid seed/state policies equal those generated by completed prefix kernels $q_t\in L^\infty(\lambda_t;\Delta(G))$, where
$$
L_\theta(q)=\sum_{t=1}^T\sum_{a\in G}\int q_{t,a}\,d\mu^\theta_{t,a}.
$$

Combined with the existing weak-* compactness proof for $L^\infty$ kernels, this gives the payoff-measure dominated no-gap theorem for the original common Borel policy model.

## Claims Or Lemmas

**Lemma 1: $C_{\rm Borel}\subseteq C_{L^\infty}$.**  
Under the common Borel seed/state model, every policy induces Borel public-prefix marginals $q_{t,a}(h)$ preserving all expected reward coordinates.

**Lemma 2: $C_{L^\infty}\subseteq C_{\rm Borel}$.**  
Every completed $L^\infty(\lambda_t;\Delta(G))$ kernel has a Borel simplex-valued representative $\tilde q_t$ equal $\lambda_t$-a.e.; finite-action uniform randomization implements $\tilde q_t$, and domination preserves all reward coordinates.

**Lemma 3: Product compactness transfers to Borel policies.**  
Since $C_{\rm Borel}=C_{L^\infty}$ and $C_{L^\infty}$ is product compact, finite-subclass ordinary-prior values have no infinite-class gap under payoff-measure domination.

## Proof Attempts

Forward inclusion:

A common Borel seed/state policy with iid private uniforms gives Borel maps
$$
a_t=\alpha_t(P_{1:t},U_{1:t})
$$

by induction, because internal state, past actions, and delayed feedback are Borel functions of public prices and private seeds. Define
$$
q_{t,a}(h)=\Pr_U(\alpha_t(h,U_{1:t})=a).
$$

Section-integral measurability makes $q_{t,a}$ Borel. Fubini and independence of $U$ from prices give
$$
L_\theta(A)
=
\sum_{t,a}\int q_{t,a}(h)\,d\mu^\theta_{t,a}(h).
$$

Reverse inclusion:

Take $q_t\in L^\infty(\lambda_t;\Delta(G))$. Coordinatewise completed-measurable-to-Borel versions give Borel $b_{t,a}$ equal to $q_{t,a}$ $\lambda_t$-a.e. Redefine on the Borel null set where $b_t\notin\Delta(G)$ to a fixed vertex. Then $\tilde q_t:X_t\to\Delta(G)$ is Borel and equals $q_t$ $\lambda_t$-a.e.

Because $\mu^\theta_{t,a}\ll\lambda_t$,
$$
\int \tilde q_{t,a}\,d\mu^\theta_{t,a}
=
\int q_{t,a}\,d\mu^\theta_{t,a}
$$

simultaneously for all $\theta,t,a$. Implement $\tilde q_t$ with a fresh uniform and cumulative intervals over finite $G$.

## Gaps And Risks

The final write-up still needs to cite or prove three standard facts: completed-measurable functions on standard Borel spaces have Borel versions modulo a sigma-finite Borel measure; Borel section integrals are Borel; finite-action kernels are implementable by Borel maps plus independent uniforms.

The theorem fails outside the exact model: law-by-law a.s. policies, law-correlated private randomness, private side information, action-dependent prices, nonadditive rewards, inventory constraints, or continuum quote kernels without discretization.

The equality is not behavioral. Borelization may change actions on prefix-law-positive but payoff-$\lambda_t$-null histories. This is harmless only because the theorem tracks additive expected payoff coordinates.

## Counterexamples Or Obstructions

The completed-kernel null-set stress test remains the main wording hazard: payoff domination does not imply prefix-law domination, so one must not claim pathwise or feedback-state equivalence.

The non-Borel label construction remains the obstruction to arbitrary infinite-class no-gap theorems without domination/product closure.
