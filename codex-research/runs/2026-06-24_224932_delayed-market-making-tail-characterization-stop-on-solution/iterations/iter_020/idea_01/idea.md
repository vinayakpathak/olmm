# Referee-Proof ba Dual

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Write the finite-grid arbitrary-class ba dual as a standalone theorem: define the common randomized policy class, C subset l-infinity(Theta), norm closure, separation from C-X_+, positivity of the separator, normalization to ba_1^+, and the reverse weak-duality inequality. Track whether the theorem gives value equality only or also approximate policies.

## Why Promising
The iteration 20 solver leans on this dual but does not prove it. A clean proof would secure the strongest exact arbitrary-class value statement and clarify exactly what remains unsolved.

## Risks
May only confirm an abstract tautological value identity; no tail or rate content. The proof depends sensitively on the chosen policy class and on ex-ante randomization making C convex.
