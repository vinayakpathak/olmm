# Finalize delta upper theorem

ID: idea_02

## Summary
Turn the comparator-gap truncation result into a polished theorem for unknown exogenous classes: regret bounded by sqrt(T H log K)+T/K+sup_P delta_T^P(H). Include exact quantifiers for horizon-indexed classes, one uniform algorithm, grid discretization, and delayed full-information feedback.

## Why Promising
This is the strongest accepted upper-bound route and subsumes the older beta-based sufficient condition.

## Risks
This may remain only sufficient; the delayed-experts theorem still needs an exact statement or proof.

- proof strategy
- rate optimization
