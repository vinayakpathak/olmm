# Audit Compact-Menu Local Lemma

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Ask an explorer to fully prove or break the compact-menu local theorem. For compact C, m=max C, successor s_C(x)=min(C cap [x,m]), and delta(C) defined by integral_delta^m dx/s_C(x)=1, verify existence, uniqueness, measurability, the primal distribution with density dx/s_C(x), and a dual threshold law supported on C giving the matching lower bound. Stress-test singleton menus, finite menus, Cantor-like menus, and menus accumulating at 0.

## Why Promising
This is the main new solver contribution and would naturally become the infinite-menu analogue of the accepted finite-menu formula.

## Risks
The proposed lower-bound survival law may fail at jump or accumulation points; exact attainment is impossible under strict crossing, so the theorem must be phrased as an infimum or eta-implementation.

## Suggested Lenses
- proof strategy
- measure-theoretic audit
- counterexample search
