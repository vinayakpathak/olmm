# Pin Down The Nontrivial Quantifiers

ID: idea_01

## Summary
Formalize the solver's quantifier-collapse observation: if the learner may depend on both the process law and the horizon, every process is learnable by repeatedly playing a horizon-optimal fixed quote up to an epsilon. Then separate the nontrivial regimes: horizon-oblivious process-aware learners, universal learners over a class, minimax learners, and post-history uniform guarantees.

## Why Promising
It may completely settle which readings of the original iff are impossible before explorers spend effort proving necessity statements that are vacuous or false.

## Risks
This may mainly clarify definitions rather than produce a new theorem; horizon-oblivious process-aware learnability may still collapse under a different oracle argument.

## Suggested Lenses
- definition audit
- proof strategy
- counterexample search
